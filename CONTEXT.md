# Transcriber

The transcriber turns a recording into a transcript that says who spoke, when,
and what they said, for anyone who needs a written record of what was said.

## Language

**Recording**  
An audio or video file in which people speak, given to the transcriber to be
transcribed. It may come from a meeting, a call, an interview or any other
occasion.  
_Avoid_: meeting, audio

**Transcript**  
The text the transcriber produces from one recording: what was said, when it
was said, measured from the start of the recording, and who said it.  
_Avoid_: minutes

**Speaker**  
A person whose voice is heard in a recording, whether or not the transcriber
can tell who they are.  
_Avoid_: participant

**Recognition**  
Matching a speaker's voice with a person in the voice library, so that the
transcript shows that person's name. When the match is not sure enough, the
speaker stays anonymous.  
_Avoid_: identification

**Anonymous speaker**  
A speaker whom recognition did not name, shown in the transcript as
"Persona N" until someone names them.  
_Avoid_: unknown speaker

**Naming**  
A person giving a name to an anonymous speaker.

**Unattributed speech**  
Words the transcriber heard but could not assign to any speaker, shown in the
transcript as "Voce non attribuita".

**Voice library**  
The people the transcriber can recognize by name on one PC, each represented by
voice clips of their own.  
_Avoid_: enrollment

**Voice clip**  
A short audio file in which only one person speaks, kept in a voice library so
that the transcriber can recognize that person.  
_Avoid_: enrollment clip

**Pool**  
A group of one person's voice clips that recognition compares as one, through
their average: the clips directly in the person's folder, or those in one of
its subfolders, such as a folder for one recording condition.

**Voiceprint**  
What the transcriber derives from a person's voice clips to recognize their
voice.  
_Avoid_: embedding

**Recognition check**  
A test of recognition on one PC: it replays that PC's named recordings against
its own voice library and reports which speakers the current settings would
name wrongly or leave anonymous.

**Listening check**  
The step of naming in which the person hears the best voice clips of a speaker
and confirms whose voice it is, before any clip joins the voice library.

**Trial period**  
The time during which every clip cut from a named speaker is kept, until the
person's deletions show that the transcriber's own choice of clips can be
trusted.

**Agent**  
An AI coding assistant, such as Claude Code or Codex, that uses the transcriber
on behalf of a person.

**Maintainer**  
The person who builds and distributes the transcriber, helps the people who use
it, and tunes how it recognizes voices.  
_Avoid_: pilot manager
