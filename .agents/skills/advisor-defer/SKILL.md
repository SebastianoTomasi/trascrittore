Deferred items preserve future work; they are not temporary conversation
handoffs or substitutes for inputs needed by the current decision. Follow
[deferred-items.md](deferred-items.md) whenever capturing, inspecting,
classifying, resuming, or closing one.

Record an already-clear question, idea, or other future concern directly. If
the input does not yet identify what should be revisited and provide either a
possible outcome or a meaningful revisit condition, use `advisor-grill-me` for
a short, one-question-at-a-time clarification before writing. Do not reopen a
clear, already-agreed deferral merely to record it.

Before recommending work or starting or resuming an advisor session, recover
the relevant records through that procedure. Advice-only work reads them
without changing them. When a deferred item is resolved, put the authoritative
answer through the normal `advisor-grill-with-docs` acceptance procedure, or
`advisor-propagate-change` when it changes an already accepted rule, before
closing the record.
